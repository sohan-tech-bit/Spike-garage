# Product: Spike Garage

A browser-based 3D car customizer with a real horror theme. The user walks into an abandoned, fog-filled garage at night, picks a car, and spray-paints and stickers it in fine detail. The car is theirs to corrupt: rust, blood drips, cobwebs, cursed symbols, glowing eyes.

Hackathon: Kiroween 2026, **Costume Contest** category (haunting, polished, unforgettable UI that serves the function).

## Rules we never break
- **No real brand logos, badges or trademarks**, anywhere (cars, stickers, UI, video, README).
- **Cars use generic invented names** (e.g. "The Hearse", "Night Coupe", "Rust Wagon", "Phantom Hatch"). Never real manufacturer or model names.
- Only CC0 / properly licensed 3D models and assets. Record the source and license of every asset in `ASSETS.md`.
- No copyrighted music or sound. Use CC0 or self-made audio only.
- Sticker packs are original art: bats, skulls, ravens, flames, cobweb corners, eyes, runes, blood splats, ghost tags.

## Users
Horror fans, car-culture fans, and hackathon judges watching a 3-minute demo. Everything must be understandable in seconds.

## Priorities (in order)
1. Spray painting on the car surface feels great and works reliably.
2. Stickers: place, scale, rotate, layer, delete.
3. Horror atmosphere (fog, flicker, sound, UI) that enhances the tool, never blocks it.
4. Undo/redo, clear, save PNG.
5. Extras only after 1-4 are solid: wheel/window options, share link, photo mode.

## Tone
Dread, not gore for its own sake. Atmospheric, decaying, candle and flicker-light. Keep it suitable for a general audience (no graphic violence imagery in assets).
